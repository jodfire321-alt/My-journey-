29 SEPTEMBER 2026

Engineering College Shahdol Visit

29 September 2026 ka din mere liye bahut khaas aur yaadgaar raha. Us din hamare vidyalay dwara humein Engineering College, Shahdol ke shaikshanik bhraman par le jaya gaya. Is bhraman ka uddeshya humein Engineering College ke vatavaran, prayogshalon, pustakalay tatha wahan hone wali vibhinn gatividhiyon ke baare mein jaankari dena tha. Subah hum sabhi vidyarthi apne vidyalay pahunche aur wahan se bus dwara Engineering College ke liye rawana hue. Bus mein doston ke saath yatra karne mein bahut maza aaya. Raaste mein hum sabhi aapas mein baatein karte hue aur hansi-mazaak karte hue college pahunche. College pahunchne ke baad hum sabhi bahut utsahit the, kyunki humein pehli baar itne bade takneeki sansthan ko kareeb se dekhne ka avsar mil raha tha. College ke mukhya dwar se andar pravesh karne ke baad humne wahan ki kuch tasveerein bhi leen. Iske baad hamare shikshakon aur college ke margdarshakon ne humein college ke baare mein mahatvapurn jaankari di. Sabse pehle humein pustakalay le jaya gaya. Wahan bahut saari kitaabein dekhkar humein padhai aur gyaan ke mahatva ka ehsaas hua. Iske baad humein Chemistry Lab, Physics Lab aur Computer Lab dikhaye gaye. Prayogshalon mein rakhe upkaranon aur wahan hone wale prayogon ke baare mein humein jaankari di gayi. Computer Lab mein aadhunik takneek aur computer se sambandhit kai cheezein dekhne ko mili. Yah anubhav hamare liye bahut gyaanvardhak raha. Iske baad humein college ki Workshop bhi dikhai gayi. Wahan machineon aur takneeki upkaranon ke baare mein bataya gaya. Humein yah samajhne ka avsar mila ki Engineering ki padhai keval kitaabon tak seemit nahi hoti, balki usmein vyavaharik gyaan ka bhi bahut mahatva hota hai. Bhraman ke dauran college ki or se humein naashta bhi diya gaya. Doston ke saath baithkar naashta karna aur poore din ki gatividhiyon ko yaad karna bahut achha laga. Is poore bhraman ke dauran humne bahut kuch naya seekha. Humein Engineering College ka vatavaran dekhne, prayogshalon ko samajhne aur wahan ki padhai ke baare mein jaanne ka avsar mila. Is yatra ne mere man mein Engineering aur takneeki shiksha ke prati ruchi aur bhi badha di. Ant mein main apne vidyalay ke sabhi shikshakon ka dhanyavaad karna chahta hoon, jinhone humein is shaikshanik bhraman ka avsar diya. Yah yatra mere liye keval manoranjan ka saadhan nahi thi, balki seekhne, dekhne aur apne bhavishya ke baare mein sochne ka ek mahatvapurn anubhav thi.
✨ "Yah bhraman mere liye ek aisi yaadgaar yatra rahi, jisne mujhe nai cheezein seekhne ke saath-saath apne bhavishya ke sapnon ko aur spasht roop se dekhne ki prerna di."
